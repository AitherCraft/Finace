                  Customize Home

                  Drag a card by the ☰ handle to move it, or use the arrows. Tap the eye to show or hide a card. Then tap Save settings. The summary tiles stay at the top.

                    Notifications

                    Reminders show up here and as alerts on this device. They are checked when you open RefVault and while it is open. On iPhone, add RefVault to your Home Screen first, then turn this on.

                    Weather

                    Shows the forecast on Home and the weather for each upcoming game, with tips like bringing a rain jacket. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo. Your location is only used to look up the weather and is kept on this device.

                    Seasonal mode

                    RefVault spots your off-season from your games (a long gap with nothing coming up, or months that were quiet in past years) and slows your savings forecasts to match. If you entered season dates under Associations, those are used instead. Override it here, then tap Save settings.

                    My widgets

                    Build your own Home widgets from the info in the app. Pick what to show, name it, choose an icon and color. Then tap Save settings.

                    Extra security

                    Type of extra security

                    On: the PIN or Face ID is asked every time you switch away and come back. Off: it only asks when the app is opened fresh. This setting is for this device.

                    The PIN is asked when you open the app and, unless you turn that off below, every time you leave it. It is stored scrambled, never as the numbers you typed. It locks the screen on this app. It does not replace your account password.

                    The code is a 6 digit number sent from RefVault. It lasts 10 minutes. Check spam if it does not show up.
